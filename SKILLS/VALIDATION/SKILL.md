# Validation Skill

## Purpose
Convert an idea or product hypothesis into evidence strong enough for a business decision.

## Workflow
Hypothesis -> cheapest test -> qualified targets -> offer -> approved outreach -> payment/usage evidence -> analysis -> decision.

## Evidence ladder
1. payment
2. repeated paid use/renewal
3. explicit purchase intent with a concrete next step
4. qualified positive response
5. free usage
6. compliments/likes

Do not treat lower-level evidence as equivalent to payment.

## Decision rules
- strong validation -> proceed
- partial validation -> refine and retest
- no meaningful validation -> stop, change offer or test another hypothesis

Record the reason for every decision.
