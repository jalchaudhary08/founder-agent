# Analytics Skill

## Purpose
Measure whether a product and business are actually improving.

## Track where relevant
- acquisition
- activation
- conversion
- paid conversion
- retention
- churn
- usage frequency
- support load
- latency
- error rate
- AI cost per job
- gross margin per job

## Rules
Define metric formulas before interpreting them. Distinguish counts from rates. Do not infer causation from correlation alone.
