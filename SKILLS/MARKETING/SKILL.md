# Marketing Skill

## Purpose
Acquire customers by communicating a real value proposition to a relevant audience.

## Workflow
Customer -> painful problem -> promise -> proof -> channel -> experiment -> measurement.

## Outreach
Research first. Personalize from verified public facts. Do not fabricate familiarity or urgency. Do not mass-send without approval.

## Measurement
Track replies, qualified interest, demos, payments and retention separately. Impressions are not revenue.
