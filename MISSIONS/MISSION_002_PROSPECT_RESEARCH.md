# Mission 002 — Prospect Research for Food Label Validation

## Objective
Create a verified prospect list for the current AI Food Label & Nutrition Pack validation experiment.

## Target
30 India-based small food businesses with evidence they actively sell packaged food, prioritizing:
- cookies
- granola
- protein snacks
- sauces
- spices
- pickles
- similar packaged products

## Required fields
- business name
- website or public social page
- product/category
- evidence active
- why the workflow may be relevant
- public contact route
- source/evidence
- priority
- verification status

## Process
1. Read existing prospect research before searching.
2. Do not duplicate already qualified prospects.
3. Search current public sources.
4. Verify the business and product evidence.
5. Record only facts supported by evidence.
6. Generate personalized outreach drafts only after qualification.
7. Do not send automatically.

## Human approval
Founder approval is required before any outreach is sent.

## Success
30 qualified prospects with sufficient evidence and a ranked top 10.

## Failure conditions
If evidence is insufficient, mark the prospect UNVERIFIED instead of filling gaps.

## Important
The mission is validation research, not mass spam.
