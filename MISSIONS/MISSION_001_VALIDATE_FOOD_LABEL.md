# Mission 001 — Validate AI Food Label & Nutrition Pack

## Objective
Find evidence that real small food businesses will pay $9.99 for a useful nutrition/label preparation workflow.

## Do not
- build the full SaaS yet
- invent prospects
- invent contact details
- send outreach automatically
- claim regulatory compliance

## Prospect target
Small food businesses, especially:
- packaged cookies
- granola
- protein snacks
- sauces
- spices
- pickles
- small packaged-food brands

## Research target
30 qualified prospects.

For each prospect capture:
- business name
- website/social
- product evidence
- evidence active
- why the workflow may matter
- public contact route
- source
- priority

## Outreach
Create personalized drafts after qualification. Human approval is required before sending.

## Success criteria
- 2–3 unrelated genuine payments -> proceed to MVP
- 1 payment -> refine and retest
- 0 payments -> diagnose, revise offer or test another opportunity

## Evidence standard
A payment is strong validation. Positive comments, likes, surveys and free usage are not equivalent to payment.
