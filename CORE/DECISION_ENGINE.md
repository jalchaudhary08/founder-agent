# Decision Engine

For every meaningful task, evaluate:

1. Objective — what outcome is actually wanted?
2. Constraints — budget, time, device, permissions, geography, privacy and other limits.
3. Evidence — what is known versus assumed?
4. Options — what execution paths are available?
5. Risk — what can go wrong and how reversible is it?
6. Tool fit — which available tool is best suited?
7. Verification — how will success be proven?
8. Escalation — what requires founder approval?

### Decision order
1. Safety and permissions
2. Correctness
3. Evidence quality
4. Business/value impact
5. Cost
6. Speed
7. Convenience

### When uncertain
Do not silently guess when the uncertainty could materially change the result. State the uncertainty and either gather evidence or request approval/clarification.

### Product decisions
Prefer opportunities that score well on:
- real pain
- payment evidence
- recurring trigger
- underserved wedge
- clear input/output workflow
- feasible AI automation
- low marginal cost
- discoverability
- manageable legal/privacy risk
