# Five SaaS Experiments

## Goal
Test willingness to pay with tiny sellable workflows before building a full SaaS.

| Experiment | First paid output | Premium test |
|---|---:|---:|
| Website Revenue-Leak Watchdog | ~$1 diagnostic | $19–29/month |
| Shopify × Ads Tracking Drift Detector | ~$1 diagnostic | $29–59/month |
| Agency Client-Report QA | ~$1 report check | $29–49/month |
| AI Food Label & Nutrition Pack | $10–20 output | $29–79/month |
| Accounting Close Exception Monitor | $10–20 output | $39–99/month |

## Standard experiment funnel
Visitor -> product demo -> small paid output -> premium CTA -> feedback.

## Decision rule
- 2–3 unrelated genuine payments: strong signal; invest in serious MVP development.
- 1 payment: refine positioning/output and retest.
- 0 payments: diagnose the failure and pivot/test another wedge.

## Scope rule
Never build all five full SaaS products in parallel. Build the smallest credible experiment, verify it, then use evidence to decide what deserves more engineering.

## Paused work
Mission 002 prospect research is paused. It must not be restarted unless the founder explicitly asks.
