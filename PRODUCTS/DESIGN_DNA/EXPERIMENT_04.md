# Experiment 04 Design DNA — AI Food Label & Nutrition Pack

## Identity
Product: LABELKIT
Category: food-product preparation workspace
Personality: clean, ingredient-led, precise, packaging-studio calm.

## Visual metaphor
A modern food packaging proof sheet: ingredient strips, nutrition panel, batch/version marks and review stamps.

## Audience
Small packaged-food brands and product-content operators.

## Anxiety
"I have the ingredients, but turning them into a consistent label-ready pack takes too much time."

## Visual system
- Cream paper: #F7F2E8
- Ink: #1E211D
- Tomato red: #C84B36
- Olive: #657047
- Mustard: #C49332
- Leaf green: #47735A
- Rule: #D6CEC0

No neon purple, robots, glowing AI brains, generic SaaS gradients or fake food photography.

## Layout
Packaging proof-board: ingredient ledger on the left, nutrition panel in the center, review/flags rail on the right.

## Hero
"Turn product data into a label-ready first draft."
Subline: "Nutrition, ingredients, allergen flags and product copy in one reviewable pack."

## Interaction
Static sample → structured product input → payment gate → deterministic nutrition aggregation → generated draft pack → human review checklist.

## Motion
Subtle paper/label transitions, nutrition numbers counting only when meaningful, flag stamps. Reduced-motion mode removes transitions.

## Trust
Show input provenance, calculation method, assumptions, allergen limitations and "not regulatory approval" language prominently.

## CTA
"Build the $15 Product Pack"

## Mobile
Nutrition panel and ingredient ledger stack vertically. Flags remain visible before the final CTA.

## Accessibility/performance
Semantic labels, keyboard access, high contrast, no critical information hidden in animation, lightweight CSS, no external image dependency.
