# Experiment 02 — Design DNA

## Product
Shopify × Ads Tracking Drift Detector

## Customer
Small e-commerce operators, freelance media buyers and small agencies managing 3–15 stores.

## Anxiety
"Meta/Google says one number. Shopify says another. Which one should I trust?"

## Personality
Reconciliation desk: precise, calm, forensic, operational.

## Visual metaphor
Two ledgers sliding out of alignment, with a measured variance line between them.

## Palette
- ink: #151515
- paper: #F5F2EA
- cobalt: #2457D6
- warning amber: #C88918
- healthy green: #3C7A57
- muted red: #B44A45

No neon purple, AI blobs, robots or decorative gradients.

## UI
Split-ledger layout:
SHOPIFY | ADS PLATFORM
matched rows align; drift rows are offset and highlighted.
Hero should show a real reconciliation example, not a generic dashboard.

## Typography
Editorial sans for headings, compact tabular numerals for metrics and deltas.

## Motion
CSV drop -> rows normalize -> matching lines connect -> variance counter settles.
Reduced motion: static comparison.

## CTA
Primary: "Run the $1 Drift Check"
Secondary: "See a sample mismatch"

## Trust
Show methodology, sample inputs, assumptions, privacy/data handling and limitations.
No fake customer logos, testimonials or performance claims.
