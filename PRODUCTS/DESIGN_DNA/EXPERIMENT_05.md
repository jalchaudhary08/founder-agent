# Experiment 05 Design DNA — Accounting Close Exception Monitor

## Identity
Product: CLOSELINE
Category: month-end accounting control
Personality: controlled, exact, quiet, professional, operational.

## Visual metaphor
A closing ledger / control room: a clean ledger grid with a narrow exception rail and a large "CLEAR / REVIEW" close status.

## Audience
Bookkeepers, fractional finance teams and small accounting firms.

## Anxiety
"Which few transactions actually need my attention before I close the books?"

## Visual system
- Ledger paper: #F2F0EA
- Ink: #18201D
- Deep navy: #26384A
- Brass: #A67C32
- Exception red: #A94742
- Clear green: #4A715B
- Rule: #C9C6BE

No neon purple, robots, glowing AI objects, crypto aesthetics, fake bank logos or decorative 3D.

## Layout
Dense but readable ledger: close-status header, KPI strip, exception queue, evidence rows and review actions.

## Hero
"Close the books. Not your eyes."
Subline: "Find the transactions worth reviewing before month-end close."

## Interaction
Static sample → CSV selection → payment gate → deterministic checks → exception queue → human review.

## Motion
Very restrained: row highlights and status counter changes. Reduced motion removes transitions.

## Trust
Explain each rule, show evidence, confidence/context and limitations. Clearly distinguish anomaly from fraud/error.

## CTA
"Run the $15 Close Check"

## Mobile
KPI strip becomes horizontal cards; exception queue stacks; evidence rows become readable key-value blocks.

## Accessibility/performance
Semantic table structure where practical, keyboard controls, strong contrast, no critical information hidden behind hover, lightweight CSS, CSV-only MVP.
