# AI Food Label & Nutrition Pack

## Positioning
A workflow that turns a small food business's recipe/ingredient and available source information into a structured nutrition and label-preparation pack.

## Target customer
India-based small packaged-food businesses that sell products such as cookies, granola, protein snacks, sauces, spices, pickles, and similar packaged foods.

## Intended input
- recipe
- ingredient list
- quantities
- serving information
- available lab/source data

## Intended work
- parse ingredients
- calculate/structure nutrition where source data supports it
- identify allergens
- prepare ingredient declaration
- identify missing information
- produce a label-ready document
- preserve evidence/source information

## Output
- nutrition information
- ingredient declaration
- allergen identification
- serving information
- label-ready document
- missing-data warnings
- evidence/source information

## Important limitation
The product must not promise universal legal or regulatory compliance. Regulatory requirements vary and uncertain fields must be flagged for review.
