# Experiment 04 — AI Food Label & Nutrition Pack

Status: BUILDING

## Problem
Small food brands and product teams repeatedly turn ingredient/product data into nutrition summaries, allergen callouts, ingredient-list formatting and product copy. Manual preparation is repetitive and error-prone.

## Wedge
A structured product-data-to-label-pack workflow, not a generic AI food chatbot. The system shows calculated values, source inputs, assumptions and flags so a human can review before publication.

## Target customer
Small packaged-food brands, home/indie food businesses moving toward packaged products, nutrition/product-content freelancers, and small agencies serving food brands.

## MVP
1. Enter ingredient/product data using a simple structured form.
2. Produce a sample label pack without any external AI/API cost.
3. After payment verification, process real submitted data.
4. Calculate/aggregate nutrition values from supplied ingredient rows where values are provided.
5. Generate ingredient-list draft, allergen flags based only on declared ingredients, nutrition summary, product-description draft and review checklist.
6. Clearly separate calculated facts from AI/drafting suggestions and assumptions.

## Paid test
First output: $10–20 product pack.

## Premium hypothesis
$29–79/month for saved products, recurring label packs, version history, exports, team review and product libraries.

## Non-goals
- Regulatory certification
- Medical/dietary advice
- Guaranteed FDA/FSSAI/EU compliance
- Lab testing
- Nutrition database licensing
- Automatic legal approval
- Generic food chatbot

## Trust
Never invent nutrition values when inputs are absent. Every calculated value must trace to supplied inputs. Allergen flags are screening signals, not legal certification. Final label/compliance review remains human responsibility.
