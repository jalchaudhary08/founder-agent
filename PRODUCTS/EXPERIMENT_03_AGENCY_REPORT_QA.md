# Experiment 03 — Agency Client-Report QA

Status: BUILDING

## Problem
Agencies and freelance media buyers can send client reports with mismatched totals, inconsistent date ranges, missing metrics, duplicate rows, broken percentages, or suspicious jumps.

## Wedge
A pre-send quality-control desk that answers: "Is this client report internally consistent enough to send?"

## Target
Small performance-marketing agencies, freelance media buyers, and reporting specialists.

## MVP
CSV-first: upload report → verify payment → inspect structure/arithmetic → return evidence, severity and prioritized fixes.

## Paid test
First output: about $1 report check.

## Premium hypothesis
$29–49/month for recurring checks, saved QA history, templates, scheduled checks and agency workspaces.

## Non-goals
Full BI platform, ad buying, guaranteed correctness, PDF/OCR ingestion, fake benchmarks/testimonials.

## Trust
Every finding must reference observable CSV structure or arithmetic. Never claim correctness beyond the checks performed.
