# Experiment 01 — Website Revenue-Leak Watchdog

## Status
DESIGN_READY / BUILD_NEXT

## Hypothesis
Small businesses and SaaS/ecommerce teams will pay a small amount for a fast diagnosis that identifies the highest-value conversion/revenue leaks on a public website and tells them exactly what to fix first.

## Wedge
This is not a generic "AI website audit".

The experiment focuses on:
- commercial leak detection
- evidence from the actual page
- prioritization by likely business impact vs effort
- a short "fix these first" action plan
- transparent assumptions instead of fabricated revenue claims

## First paid output
Target: ~$1 diagnostic.

Input:
- public website URL
- optional primary conversion goal

Output:
- concise revenue-leak score
- 3 highest-priority leaks
- evidence/location for each finding
- recommended fix
- estimated effort band
- confidence/assumptions
- clear next action

No guaranteed revenue increase. Revenue estimates are modeled scenarios only when the required inputs exist.

## Premium hypothesis
$19–29/month for:
- recurring monitoring/re-audits
- regression alerts
- issue history
- before/after comparison
- prioritized weekly action queue

The experiment should validate whether customers want the recurring workflow, not merely the one-time report.

## Validation funnel
Visitor -> paste URL -> instant useful preview -> ~$1 unlock -> paid diagnostic -> premium CTA -> feedback.

## Success gate
- 2–3 unrelated genuine payments: strong signal
- 1 payment: refine and retest
- 0 payments: diagnose/pivot

## Competition observation
Current market evidence shows multiple low-cost/free AI website/CRO audit products and higher-priced human/managed audits. The experiment must therefore win on a narrow, commercially useful output rather than "more audit criteria". citeturn0search1turn0search2turn0search18

## Non-goals
- full SEO crawler
- generic accessibility scanner
- enterprise analytics platform
- guaranteed ROI calculator
- fake customer/revenue proof
- full SaaS subscription before demand evidence

## Product truth
The tool can observe public-page evidence. It cannot know actual lost revenue without first-party business data. Any dollar impact must be explicitly labeled as a scenario/estimate.
