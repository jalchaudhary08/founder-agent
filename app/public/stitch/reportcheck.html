Agency Operations & Privacy FAQ

Does REPORTCHECK store our clients' media spend figures?

No. The audit engine operates exclusively in volatile browser memory (client-side DOM & Web Workers). The moment your browser tab or session closes, all row records, campaign titles, and expenditure totals are eradicated. We maintain zero database storage of your client data.

Is our proprietary client data fed into any AI training model?

Never. REPORTCHECK is Illustrative deterministic code written in mathematical logic algorithms and strict relational parsers. We do not utilize Large Language Models (LLMs) or generative models for audit processing, eliminating hallucination risks and ensuring zero IP leaks.

How does this integrate with Looker Studio or Supermetrics?

You can drop raw CSV exports directly from any reporting tool prior to pasting into client presentation decks. On Agency and Partner plans, our Google Sheets extension and webhook APIs automatically validate tables upon sheet generation before external sharing.

What file sizes and row counts are supported?

The browser parser effortlessly processes files up to 100 MB and over 250,000 rows without interface latency. High-volume media campaigns with granular hourly breakdown files run within seconds without server throttling.

Can we customize the 42 rules for specific enterprise clients?

Yes. Agency subscribers can define custom thresholds, such as requiring ROAS to be calculated gross-of-fees, enforcing mandatory brand taxonomy naming conventions, or flagging spend deviations exceeding custom tolerance percentages (±2%).

Can we share the Pre-Flight Dispatch Seal with our clients?

Yes. Once all 42 rules pass cleanly, the desk generates an encrypted SHA-256 Pre-Flight Certificate. Top agencies append this single-page proofing badge directly to the appendix of their executive decks to demonstrate forensic quality rigor.
