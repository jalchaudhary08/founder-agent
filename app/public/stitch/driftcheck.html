Technical & Methodological FAQ

Exacting answers to nuanced questions regarding attribution windows, Apple iOS 14.5+ restrictions, and reconciliation tolerances.

RECONCILIATION DESK TECHNICAL STANDARDS
Why do ad platforms show more sales than Shopify received?

Platforms use view-through attribution (crediting purchases to users who merely viewed an ad without clicking) alongside modeled conversions (statistical estimation). If both Meta and Google claim credit for the same buyer, total platform conversions will dramatically exceed physical bank receipts.

How does Driftcheck handle iOS Safari WebKit ITP blocks?

Apple's WebKit ITP cuts first-party cookie longevity to 24 hours and blocks third-party scripts. Driftcheck identifies checkouts where order timestamps exist in Shopify but possess zero corresponding browser pixels, isolating exactly which purchases bypassed client-side tracking.

Is customer data stored or shared with external parties?

Never. Driftcheck was architected with a strict Zero-Persistence guarantee. The file parsing and 1:1 row cross-referencing run inside local ephemeral browser memory. Once your audit window closes or you hit refresh, data structures are completely zeroized.

What if my Shopify store and ad accounts operate in different currencies?

Our ingestion normalization engine detects sovereign currency differences via standard ISO-4217 tags. If you bill in GBP but run ad accounts in USD, Driftcheck recalculates transactions against verified daily European Central Bank (ECB) benchmark exchange rates.

Can Driftcheck files be imported into Google Sheets or Looker Studio?

Yes. Every reconciled output produces clean, RFC-4180 compliant CSV files with standardized discrepancy flags (`MATCHED`, `MISSING_PIXEL`, `GHOST_ATTRIBUTION`, `FX_SKEW`), allowing seamless import into your existing agency reporting pipelines.

What is your refund policy if files cannot be reconciled?

If your CSV files comply with our published schema matrix but our deterministic matching algorithm fails to parse or generate a forensic dossier, we issue an immediate Illustrative refund upon technical review by our engineering desk.
