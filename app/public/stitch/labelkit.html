Why We Refuse Generative AI in Food Label Math

In consumer food packaging, a hallucinated calcium percentage or a subtly missed peanut cross-contact alert is not an interface glitch—it is an anaphylaxis hazard and an FDA Class I product recall that bankrupts indie food brands.

Large language models routinely invent plausible-sounding nutrient numbers to satisfy queries. LABELKIT takes the opposite stand: if your formulation input lacks dietary fiber or trans fat testing, our system marks it with a high-contrast watermark: [NOT PROVIDED — USER SUPPLIED ONLY].
