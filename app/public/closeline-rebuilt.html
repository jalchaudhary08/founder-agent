From messy ledger to focused review.

Good to know

Questions, answered plainly.

Clear limitations are part of a trustworthy accounting tool.

Can I connect QuickBooks or Xero today?

No. Accounting integrations and live file processing are not connected in this preview. Do not upload real ledger files.

Are the sample findings real?

No. Every record and flag shown on this page is illustrative sample content. It is not customer data or a measured product result.

Does a flag mean fraud or an accounting error?

No. A flag is only a prompt to inspect a source record. It does not prove fraud, establish an error, or replace professional review or an audit.

Can I subscribe or pay right now?

No. The prices are proposed launch prices. Payment processing is not connected, and selecting a plan does not charge you.

Will CLOSELINE change my books automatically?

The intended product is a review aid. Any future write-back capability would need explicit controls and user confirmation; it is not available now.

When will live processing be available?

There is no verified launch date to share yet. We will not display a date or capability as available until it has been implemented and tested.
