Questions

Before you trust a close report.

Is this connected to QuickBooks or Xero?

No live accounting integration is connected in this preview. Any supported-format examples are illustrative and must not be read as verified integrations.

Can I upload my real ledger?

Do not upload sensitive financial records to this preview. Real file processing and secure storage controls have not been enabled.

Are the sample exceptions real findings?

No. Sample rows and exception counts are illustrative interface examples, not findings from a customer's ledger.

Can I pay or start a subscription now?

No. The displayed prices are proposed pricing. Checkout, payment verification, and subscription management are not connected, so these buttons will not charge you.

Does a flagged item prove fraud or an accounting error?

No. An anomaly is only a prompt for human review. It is not proof of fraud, an audit opinion, or a substitute for a qualified accounting professional.
