Engineering & Governance

Methodology: Why deterministic verification beats generative guesses.

Large language models hallucinate tax laws and fabricate transaction balances. CLOSELINE relies on deterministic accounting assertions, clear boundary limitations, and human controller accountability.

Deterministic Rules

Every exception maps to an exact logical assertion: equal hash strings, date boundary windows, or standard deviation thresholds. The engine produces the exact same outcome on run #1 as run #10,000.

Zero black-box training drift

Explicit Limitations

CLOSELINE cannot verify whether a vendor invoice was physically signed or legally legitimate in court. It monitors mathematical, temporal, and metadata consistency across reported transactions.

Transparent tool boundaries

Human-in-the-Loop Sign-Off

Software surfaces the discrepancy; qualified accounting personnel make the adjustment. Every flagged item requires a documented disposition before close packet export.

Enforces segregation of duties

Core Validation Assertions Catalog

RULE #01: DUP_EXACT

Same vendor, same dollar amount, identical check/invoice reference.

RULE #02: DUP_WINDOW

Same dollar amount within 72h window under alternate payment rail.

RULE #03: DATE_POST_GAP

Posting date differs from document date by > 45 calendar days.

RULE #04: SUSPENSE_NONZERO

Suspense or Clearing account closing with balance ≠ $0.00.

Data Privacy & Sovereignty

Strictly verifiable claims. No fabricated badges.

Accounting ledgers contain sensitive payroll, vendor banking, and revenue details. We do not make unsubstantiated claims or display unverifiable security seals. Here are our exact data mechanics.

TRANSIENT IN-MEMORY EXECUTION

No Database Storage of Financial Line Items

When you run an audit pass, transaction data is parsed and evaluated in ephemeral runtime memory. Transactions are discarded upon report generation unless you explicitly save a persistent audit binder.

NO AI MODEL TRAINING

Your Books Never Train External Models

Because our core rules are deterministic heuristics and mathematical validations, customer data is never piped into external LLM training loops or third-party generative datasets.

TRANSPORT-LAYER ENCRYPTION

Enforced TLS 1.3 in Transit

All data transmitted between your browser and our edge validation workers is encrypted with TLS 1.3 using modern elliptic-curve cipher suites.

LOCAL REDACTION COMPATIBILITY

Optional Client-Side Anonymization

Need to mask employee payroll names? CLOSELINE supports pre-flight client-side tokenization, hashing employee identifiers before ingestion while preserving anomaly detection.
